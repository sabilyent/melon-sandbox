Core Features

Everything you need
to cause chaos

A full physics sandbox built in pure JavaScript — no plugins, no install, just open and play.

⚡

Real-time Physics

60 FPS impulse-based physics with verlet integration. Bodies collide, stack, and tumble with satisfying weight.

🧑‍🦯

Ragdoll Characters

Humanoid ragdolls with hinge-jointed limbs. Watch them flop, spin, and react to every explosion realistically.

💥

Explosions & Forces

Radial blast forces, screen shake, and particle bursts. Every explosion feels devastating.

🖱️

Grab & Interact

Click to grab any object, throw it, pin it, freeze it. Full mouse-driven interaction with every entity.

🔫

Arsenal of Weapons

Pistols, rifles, rockets, grenades, swords, bats — each with unique physics and force profiles.

💾

Save Your Scenes

Save your best contraptions to localStorage and reload them anytime. Share setups via JSON export.
